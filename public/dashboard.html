Using Convai AI

The MakerSpace Digital Twin features an advanced AI Assistant powered by Convai. You can interact with the environment and get information through natural language.

T
Voice Interaction

Hold T to speak. Release to send your voice command.

↵
Text Interaction

Press Enter to open the chat box, type your prompt, and press Enter again.

Example Prompts

  • "What is the current status of the Bambu D6 printer?"
  • "Can you tell me about the safety protocols for the CNC machine?"
  • "Where is the nearest fire extinguisher?"
  • "Who is currently authorized to use the Laser Cutter?"

AI Actions

The AI assistant can perform physical actions within the digital twin environment:

● "Show me..."

The AI will walk to the specified machine and highlight it for you.

Prompt: "Show me Bambu D6 printer"
● "Move to..."

Instruct the AI to navigate to a specific area or workstation.

Prompt: "Move to the assembly table"
● "Activate dashboard"

Open the detailed data view for a machine through voice command.

Prompt: "Activate dashboard for the CNC Mill"
